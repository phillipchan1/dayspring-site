## Before anything else, take your writing with you

[Download a backup](/help/download-a-backup) — Settings → Import & backup →
**Download backup**. You get a `.zip` containing every entry as plain text and
every photo as a real image file.

Do this first. Deletion is permanent, and we can't get it back for you
afterwards. Even if you're certain, a backup costs you thirty seconds and one
folder.

## Cancel the subscription first

If you're paying, [cancel the subscription](/help/managing-your-subscription)
before asking for deletion. Deleting the account doesn't cancel an Apple or
Stripe subscription — those live with Apple and Stripe, and they'll keep
charging.

## Asking for deletion

There's no delete button in the app yet. Email
**[hello@usedayspring.app](mailto:hello@usedayspring.app)** from the address on
your account, with "Delete my account" in the subject.

We'll confirm it's really you, then delete it. Expect it done **within seven
days**, and you'll get a message when it is.

## What gets deleted

Your entries, your photos, everything built from them — the Lamp, the Altar, the
Ascent — and your account itself.

Some records have to survive: a payment we're legally required to keep a record
of, for instance. Those are billing records, not your writing.

## What we can't do

We can't delete an account we can't confirm you own. If you've lost access to
the email address, say so and we'll work out another way to verify it — but we
won't delete someone's journal on an unverified request.
